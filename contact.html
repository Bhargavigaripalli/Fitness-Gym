Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the APEX membership policies and training facilities.

Can I access the facility at any hour of the day?

Yes, all APEX memberships include full 24/7 keyless biometric entry. Your fingerprint scan will grant access through our secure entry doors outside normal reception staffing hours.

Are team classes included in the pricing packages?

Group classes and scheduled training activities are included in our Performance and Elite membership tiers. Basic tier members can join classes by paying a drop-in fee of $15 per session.

Is there a contract lock-in period?

We offer month-to-month arrangements with zero lock-in contracts on all tiers. You can cancel or pause your subscription at any time with a standard 10-day notice written to info@apexperformance.com.

Do you offer facilities for showers and lockers?

Yes, our facility is equipped with clean changing rooms, private security lockers, and walk-in hot showers complete with shampoo and towel service for all active members.
