Understanding the Metrics

Body Mass Index (BMI)

BMI is a quick method used to categorize a person's weight into health bands. While useful for general populations, it does not differentiate between fat mass and skeletal muscle mass.

  • < 18.5: Underweight (Target calorie surplus)
  • 18.5 - 24.9: Normal (Maintain and condition)
  • 25.0 - 29.9: Overweight (Target calorie deficit)
  • > 30.0: Obese (Prioritize cardiovascular training)

Basal Metabolic Rate & TDEE

Your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) represents the calories your body burns at complete rest to stay alive. Your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) adjusts BMR for daily physical exercise levels.

To trigger healthy body transformation, we recommend adjusting your daily intake relative to your TDEE:

  • Caloric Deficit: Consume 300-500 kcal below TDEE for healthy weight loss.
  • Caloric Surplus: Consume 200-400 kcal above TDEE for lean muscle hypertrophy.
